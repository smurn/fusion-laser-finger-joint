# Privacy Policy — Laser Finger Joint

*Last updated: 2026-09-26*

Laser Finger Joint is an add-in for Autodesk Fusion.

**It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or usage data.**

- The add-in makes no network connections.
- It does not read or write any files of its own. The settings you enter are stored only inside your Fusion design, as parameters of the features you create, like any built-in Fusion feature.
- The dialog remembers your last-used settings in memory for the current Fusion session only; they are discarded when Fusion closes.
- Diagnostic messages are written to Fusion's own text console and log file on your computer, and are not sent anywhere by the add-in.

Autodesk's own privacy policy applies to Fusion itself and to downloads from the Autodesk App Store.
